# Lightflow Studio 1.0.0

Lightflow Studio 1.0 brings browsing, review, organization and delivery together in a stable Windows media workbench. Work from your existing folders, keep review decisions in the Catalog, and return to your workspace where you left off.

## Browse and organize

- Navigate real folders with a filesystem-first Browser. Search, filter and sort your media in Preview, Info, Hybrid or configurable Details views.
- Gather media across folders in Collections and Collection Sets. Save source-backed Smart Collections that update as their matching media changes.
- Inspect technical metadata and edit titles, descriptions, notes, creator and credit. Improved FFprobe interpretation exposes component depth and chroma consistently, including component-depth filtering.
- Copy, move, rename, delete and create folders, with larger operations tracked in Jobs.

## Review and shape your selects

- Review videos and images in the Player with filmstrip navigation, frame stepping, playback speed, looping, zoom, ratings, flags, labels and markers.
- Save a working In/Out range or multiple named Subclips without duplicating source files. Non-destructive video rotation follows the media into Previews and encoded exports.
- Jump to sampled timestamps with Visual Index; prepare indexes in the background for selected videos.
- Apply Camera and Creative `.cube` LUTs, compare looks during playback, and retain Color assignments in the Catalog. Color-aware Previews carry those choices through the review workflow.

## Deliver and continue

- Export Browser selections, saved Subclips or the current Player video with range, Color, naming, destination and encoding controls. Background Jobs keep progress and history visible while you work.
- Send source videos and native Subclips to a bin in an active Premiere Pro project through the bundled Lightflow Studio Companion.
- Restore Browser scope, filters, selection, layout and supported Player continuation, reopening video paused.
- Keep authored work in the Catalog and rebuildable data in Previews. Catalog backup-on-exit, validated recovery and long-path-safe backup/restore protection help preserve your work. Failed background probes use a persisted retry cooldown, and owned caches are excluded from source discovery.

## Installation and upgrade

- Windows 10 or Windows 11, 64-bit. Choose `LightflowStudio-1.0.0-win-x64-setup.exe` or `LightflowStudio-1.0.0-win-x64-portable.zip`.
- Packages include the .NET runtime and pinned processing/playback dependencies; separate .NET or FFmpeg installation is unnecessary. The installer requests administrator permission and installs for all users.
- NVENC H.264/HEVC export requires a supported NVIDIA GPU and driver. Catalog organization does not require NVIDIA hardware.
- Premiere handoff requires Premiere Pro 26.5 or later and the bundled Companion 1.2.2. Install it through Lightflow Settings and keep its panel open in Premiere. Color processing and Lightflow rotation are not transferred as Premiere effects. See [Premiere setup](../premiere-companion.md).
- Upgrade from the published v0.40.0 Catalog has been validated. Keep a backup before upgrading; opening upgraded storage with an older Lightflow version is not supported.
- The portable ZIP avoids installer registration but still uses local user-data locations. It does not make the Catalog shared or portable between computers. Catalog backups exclude original media and rebuildable Previews.
