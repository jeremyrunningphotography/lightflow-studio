Apple AudioQueueOutputCallback documentation:
https://developer.apple.com/documentation/audiotoolbox/audioqueueoutputcallback
Excerpt: "When the system invokes this callback, you cannot assume that the audio data from the newly-available buffer has been played."
Installed SDK independently describes buffer acquisition/reuse. Completion of buffer acquisition is not a played endpoint.
EOF must use a separate draining/completion signal; see AudioQueuePropertyListenerProc and AudioQueueStop(false).
